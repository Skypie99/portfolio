# LENS 10 — THE SKEPTIC'S WALK (banked 2026-08-07, run last, everything in hand)

**Persona:** a hostile, design-literate senior designer with 90 seconds, deciding whether this person has taste worth paying for. Chromium, both themes, phone + desktop evidence from lenses 1–9.

## What reads TEMPLATE

Structurally — almost nothing, and the skeptic will notice that absence. The scale is named, the easings are custom, the shadows are sepia, the focus rings are tokenized, the scrollbar has a hover state. The residue that still smells default: **the hamburger's three straight lines** (the one undesigned glyph in the chrome) and, if they hit ⌘P, **the browser-default print chaos** (no print layer exists — 0 rules).

## What reads AI-GENERATED

The desert plates will be clocked as AI-made by anyone fluent in 2026 render texture — and it *doesn't land as a tell here*, because the site's subject IS building with AI, the plates are graded/grained/layered like intentional cinematography, and the favicon carries a hand-authored comment describing its own composition. The skeptic pivots from "AI slop" to "art direction of AI material" inside the first scroll. The copy voice is too specific to be generated ("sized to a single careful hand"; "SEVERITY 4 · VERIFIED"). No finding — but the walk records that this question gets asked and answered.

## What they would SCREENSHOT TO MOCK

1. **The TKTK strip on the flagship** — `TKTK_ACCESSMAP_TEST_COUNT TESTS PASSING · NOT YET RECONCILED` rendering raw to strangers on the most-linked page. It is honesty-architecture mid-sentence, but the skeptic doesn't grade intent. (Known: TA-2/UP-44, Sky's words. **The single loudest cheap pixel on the estate.**)
2. **"weren't" / "Let's talk"** — typewriter apostrophes inside Cormorant-and-DM luxury prose, *inconsistently* (the 404 and one blog h2 use proper ’). The exact detail a type-literate reviewer screenshots with a red circle. (NEW → Ledger #1.)
3. **Mutual Mesh's light-theme captures glowing inside the dark room** (and its legacy un-themed unfurl) — the one project whose imagery ignores the theme system. (Known: showcase-lane asset queue.)

## What they would SCREENSHOT TO STEAL

2. **The reduced-motion register**: motion layered *onto* a resting design, and a sentence only RM readers ever receive — accessibility as a first-class aesthetic, not a compliance checkbox.

## The three moments that most say "an artist made this"

1. **The arrival** — a graded desert, one glass chip, one word (SCROLL). No pitch, total confidence.
2. **The flagship hero** — matte, plate, ledger columns (ROLE/YEAR), LIVE MAP pill: a product page composed like an exhibition wall.
3. **The dark turn** — the estate doesn't invert, it *relights*: lamplit ambers, peach accent-ink, deepened tokenized shadows, paper surfaces that keep their ink.

## The three that most betray it

1. **TKTK strip** (Sky's wording, queued).
2. **Straight apostrophes** (Ledger #1 — code-only fix).
3. **Chrome micro-finish**: default hamburger glyph + the two dead sidebar hovers + (desktop only) up to ~2s of subject-less fog before the vista resolves (the fog is inside the locked intro — banked as a question, not an item).

## The verdict, one paragraph

**They would pay — after one merge and two sentences.** In 90 hostile seconds this site does what almost no portfolio does: it stops reading as a portfolio and starts reading as a *place* with an author. The typography is a system, the motion is composed, the seams (404, favicon, unfurls, selection color) are finished to a depth that only conviction produces, and the register — candlelit gallery-atelier — is held from film to scrollbar. What keeps the verdict from being unqualified is not taste but *state*: a placeholder token on the flagship, typewriter punctuation in the prose, and ~50 commits of exactly-this-kind-of-finish sitting unmerged on a branch. The site's only visible enemies are its own unshipped fixes and four sentences only Sky can write.
